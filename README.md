An interactive, offline-first manual for teaching Ableton Live and music production concepts to beginners. Designed originally for live classroom projection and student self-study.

## 🌟 Philosophy & Blueprint
This course follows a strict architectural blueprint to maximize accessibility and pedagogical impact:

1. **Offline-First (No-Build):** All classes are self-contained HTML files with Vanilla JS & CSS. No Node.js, no Webpack, no servers. Students can copy the folder to a USB drive and open it anywhere.
2. **Projector-Friendly UI/UX:** High contrast "Ableton Dark Mode", massive typography (22px base), and strict color-coding (Audio = Blue, MIDI = Orange, Time/Play = Green).
3. **Interactive Pedagogy:** Concepts are explained through tactile browser interactions via the **Web Audio API**. Students don't just read about synthesis or routing; they click cables, trigger oscillators, and morph the timeline.
4. **Bilingual Support:** The course is maintained in both Spanish (`/es`) and English (`/en`).
